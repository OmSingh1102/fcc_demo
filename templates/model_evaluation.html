Model evaluation

Each is tested here on the client's sample cases against answers known in advance, with a confusion matrix: what the analysts decided (rows) against what the model decided (columns). Green is agreement, red is a hit cleared that the analysts did not clear, amber any other difference.

Method

What the model sees: the CRM record (name, date of birth, registered address, customer type), the passport (data page, MRZ line 1, address), the secondary ID (name, address), the proof of address, and the World-Check record (name with its aliases, listed date of birth, places, category, match score, how many profiles the name returned, the trigger).

What it never sees: the analysts' answers and what the file sets from them: the disposition, the compliance notes, the name and address variance types, the pain point, the resolution, the screening impact, the score after normalisation, the handling time, the analyst and the review date, the discrepancy analysis, the matched attributes, the note beside the listed date of birth ("Exact Match", "12 yrs diff"), and the risk rating (every true match in the file is rated High: it was set after the review).

The split: a fifth of each outcome held out, picked by a hash of the case ID, so it never changes between runs. The SOP is revised on {{ ev.split.sizes.tune }} training cases; the similar cases in the prompt come from the 800 training cases only.

Rerun: python -m fincrime.decision_model --models claude-opus-4-8,gemini-2.5-flash --examples 6 (the latest SOP unless --sop names one) · --set tune for the tuning cases · --baselines for the rules and the tree alone.
