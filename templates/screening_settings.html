AML screening (chosen under "Screen on" above)

The same key screens the person on the ID against Didit's lists: sanctions, PEP levels 1-4, warnings and enforcement, and the news if ticked. Didit scores each hit; the AI decides it under the SOP, as a World-Check hit. Test Didit AML checks the key and the credits without screening anyone, at no cost.

Aadhaar check with UIDAI (Indian Aadhaar cards)

On an Aadhaar card's case, an analyst can have UIDAI's records checked against the name, the date of birth, the full 12-digit number and the PAN, with the customer's consent (Didit's database validation, service ind_aadhaar). Didit switches database validation on after the account's first top-up; a sandbox key answers every check with a fixed full match. The case keeps the numbers masked.
