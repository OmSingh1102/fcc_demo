Lists and API

Our own sanctions screening: the official lists, downloaded free from the governments that publish them and searched here, so a name is screened without a vendor and without leaving this machine. The client's system calls it by our API, as it would call Didit's. PEP lists, the news and government registers (Aadhaar) are not on these lists: they stay with Didit or World-Check.
